About Me

I'm a data engineer at Bina Nusantara Group and an AI researcher with a growing focus on generative models and computational cognition. My thesis, “More Robust and Lighter ControlNet,” explored making diffusion-based image generation faster and more reliable — and that same curiosity now shapes my day job: building pipelines and automations that turn raw, messy data into something a system, or a person, can actually reason with. I'm especially drawn to the space where engineering discipline meets open research questions about how models, and minds, represent the world.
